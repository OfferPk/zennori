## Full Description

Immerse your mind in the tranquil elegance of **ZenNori (ノリノリ)**, a peaceful Japanese logic puzzle inspired by the revered Nikoli tradition of aesthetic brain teasers.

In ZenNori, your canvas is a handcrafted cedar bento tray partitioned into delicate chambers. Your objective is to arrange seasoned sheets of dark nori seaweed into perfectly balanced 1×2 dominoes so every chamber achieves harmony.

### The Harmony of the Rules:
1. **Chamber Balance**: Every outlined chamber must contain *exactly two* shaded nori cells.
2. **Domino Alignment**: All shaded cells must be grouped into orthogonal **1×2 dominoes** (sharing one side). Single shaded squares or polyominoes of 3+ cells are strictly forbidden.
3. **Peaceful Separation**: Dominoes may cross borders between different chambers, but **no two dominoes may touch** horizontally or vertically (diagonal contact is permitted).
4. **Mindful Deduction**: Every level is purely logical and solvable without guessing.
