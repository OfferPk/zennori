Rules of Norinori (海苔のり)

The Japanese Seaweed Domino Tradition

Norinori is an elegant pencil-and-paper logic puzzle published by Nikoli. The objective is to shade cells representing sheets of seasoned nori seaweed on a bento tray.

Core Rules

  • Chamber Rule: Each outlined chamber (bento compartment) must contain exactly two shaded cells.
  • Domino Rule: All shaded cells must be grouped into connected 1×2 dominoes (sharing an edge).
  • Separation Rule: Dominoes may cross borders between different chambers, but no two dominoes may touch horizontally or vertically (diagonal contact is permitted).
  • No Singletons or Larger Polyominoes: Shaded areas cannot be 1×1 single cells, nor 3 or more connected cells.

Controls & Tips

  • Left Click / Tap: Cycle cell through Empty ➔ Nori ➔ Dot (Unshaded).
  • Drag / Swipe: Paint nori or dots rapidly across rows and columns.
  • Chamber Counters: The faint numbers in the corners show current nori count in that chamber (turns green at 2/2).
  • Tool Selector: Switch between Cycle, Nori-only, or Dot-only tools at the bottom.
